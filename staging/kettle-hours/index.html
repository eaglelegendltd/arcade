Need help now?

If you're struggling, you don't have to wait. These are free to call from the UK. In an emergency, call 999 (or your country's emergency number) straight away.

  • Samaritans: 116 123Free, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Someone to talk to about anything.
  • NHS 111When you need urgent medical or mental health help but it isn't an emergency.
  • Childline: 0800 1111Free and confidential for anyone under 19.
  • 999 in an emergencyIf you or someone else is in danger or might act on thoughts of ending their life.

Kettle Hours is a demonstration game. It isn't medical advice, a diagnosis or certified training. The information was correct when we published it () and may have changed since, and it may not apply in your country. Always ask a GP or another medical or health professional for guidance on helping someone. Never try to diagnose anyone yourself: it can do more harm than good. Getting advice should never delay urgent help.
